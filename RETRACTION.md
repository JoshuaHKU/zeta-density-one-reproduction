# RETRACTION NOTICE / 撤稿说明

**Repository retracted (September 2026).**

This repository previously contained the preprint (v0.92) of the
critical-line program, together with its bilingual sources, Lean
artifacts and reproduction pipeline. **The central unconditional
claims of that preprint are retracted.**

## What is retracted / 撤回内容

The preprint asserted **unconditional** convergence of higher even
trace moments of the compressed Weil matrix (the mu_4 -> 13/4 tier
and above), and consequences drawn from them (the "density one"
tier). A peer reviewer's argument ("Proposition R"), verified and
strengthened by the program itself, shows that statements of this
strength cannot be established by the tools declared in the
preprint: finite unconditional bounds on higher even moments would
already force zero-free regions of power-law strength, far beyond a
century of technique, so the claimed proofs necessarily contain an
essential gap. The gap was located; the claims do not survive.

中文：本预印本关于压缩 Weil 矩阵高阶偶迹矩（mu_4 -> 13/4 档及
以上）的**无条件**收敛宣称及其推论（"密度一"档）撤回。审稿人
提出、并经本纲领自行核查强化的"命题 R"表明：以文中声明的工具
不可能证明该强度的陈述（有限的无条件高偶矩界已蕴含幂律强度的
无零区域），证明必然存在实质断点；断点已定位，宣称不成立。

## What remains valid / 仍然有效的内容

- The unconditional proportions **2/3 (optimized 0.6725) of zeros
  simple and on the critical line** are due to the independent paper
  arXiv:2608.13637 (Alpoge--Furman, verifiers), not to this
  repository, and are unaffected.
- The program's subsequent, correctly-scoped results (a two-window
  cluster-energy floor theorem; an energy-counting zero-density
  estimate with an unconditional <=4-cluster form and one explicitly
  starred lemma; a conditional simple-zeros proportion 0.6788 modulo
  the same starred lemma) are developed in separate documents with
  their hypotheses displayed, and will be released on their own
  merits.

中文：纲领后续的、范围如实标注的成果（两窗簇能量地板定理；
能量计数零密度估计，含无条件 <=4-簇形式与一颗明示星号引理；
模同一星号引理的 0.6788 简单零点比例）在独立文稿中给出，将各自
另行发布。
